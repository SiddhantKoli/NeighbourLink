POLICY DRAFT / 04

Banned items and services

Working product draft · 15 November 2026

Safety policy draft. This list is a starting point only. Counsel and safety reviewers must validate it against local laws, provider rules and actual enforcement capacity.

Do not list or request

  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances, prescription medicines, tobacco or regulated products where an unlicensed transfer is prohibited.
  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, weapons, weapon accessories or items primarily intended to injure.
  • Hazardous chemicals, toxic or radioactive materials, fuel, gas cylinders, pesticides, biological materials or unsafe electrical equipment.
  • Stolen, counterfeit, recalled, unlawfully modified, or unlicensed goods, including products that infringe intellectual property or consumer safety rules.
  • Government identity documents, payment credentials, account access, personal data, surveillance tools for covert use, or services that impersonate or unlawfully monitor another person.
  • Live animals, human remains, body parts, sexual exploitation, escort services, or any service involving abuse, coercion, discrimination or exploitation.
  • Medical, legal, financial, construction or other regulated professional services when the provider lacks required authorization or the listing would mislead a customer.
  • Any item or service whose transfer, use, collection or delivery would violate law, payment-provider rules or a safety recall.

Safe listing expectations

Owners must disclose known faults, hazards, age restrictions, protective equipment, operating instructions and required qualifications. Never ask a neighbour to bypass safety controls or use an item outside its lawful purpose. Do not share contact or identity information publicly in listing photos or descriptions.

Reporting and enforcement

Members can report listings. NeighbourLink may review, restrict or remove reported content and accounts. The admin team should document decisions and provide an appeal path. If there is an immediate danger or suspected crime, contact local emergency services and preserve relevant evidence safely.
