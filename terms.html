POLICY DRAFT / 01

Terms of use

Working product draft · 15 November 2026

Not a final legal agreement. These terms are a product draft for review by qualified counsel in every market where NeighbourLink operates. Have counsel confirm consumer, marketplace, privacy, payments, tax, safety and dispute requirements before launch.

1. What NeighbourLink does

NeighbourLink helps neighbours discover Gear to rent and Hire services offered by nearby people. Members make the rental or service agreement directly with one another. NeighbourLink provides identity, communication, evidence and payment workflow features intended to support trust. It does not own, inspect, insure or guarantee every listed item or service.

2. Accounts and eligibility

Give accurate account details, protect your sign-in credentials, and promptly report suspected account misuse. You are responsible for activity under your account. Email, phone, profile, and identity checks may be required or requested for particular features. A verification badge is limited to the checks actually completed; it is not a guarantee of safety, skill or financial responsibility.

3. Listings and member conduct

List only items and services you are authorized to offer. Describe condition, value, availability, price, pickup area, safety requirements and material limitations honestly. Use your own current photos. Keep conversations and handover records respectful and accurate. Do not use NeighbourLink for unlawful, unsafe, discriminatory, deceptive or prohibited activity.

4. Requests, rentals and services

A request is not accepted until the owner or service provider accepts it. Confirm dates, collection and return arrangements, condition, permitted use, service scope and any agreed changes in the booking conversation. Do not hand over an item before the payment state shown in the product is confirmed. Both Gear participants must submit the requested handover and return photos. Service bookings follow their own status and do not use Gear deposits.

5. Payments, deposits and fees

Where enabled, payment is processed through the configured payment provider and its approved marketplace product. NeighbourLink does not hold customer funds. A provider settlement hold is not escrow, insurance or a promise that funds can be recovered. Rental price, refundable deposit, any platform commission and applicable fees must be shown before checkout. Deposit calculations and fee settings are currently placeholders and may change after user research and legal review.

6. Returns, claims and reviews

Return items by the agreed time and condition, subject to ordinary wear. Owners may raise a supported claim during the configured claim window. The renter may respond. An authorized NeighbourLink reviewer may consider listing details, handover/return/claim photos, the conversation and booking history, then decide to return the deposit, release a supported portion to the owner, or release the deposit to the owner. The process and available remedies need counsel review before launch. Reviews must describe a genuine completed interaction.

7. Suspension and termination

NeighbourLink may restrict access when needed to investigate suspected fraud, safety risks, prohibited listings, payment issues or violations of these terms. Explain the appeal, notice and account data retention process before production launch.

8. Disclaimers and contact

NeighbourLink is provided subject to applicable law. No warranty, liability allocation, indemnity, governing law, complaint address or statutory remedy is finalized in this draft. Add the operating entity’s legal name, address and support contact after counsel review.
