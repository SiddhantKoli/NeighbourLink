POLICY DRAFT / 03

Cancellations and refunds

Working product draft · 15 November 2026

Not a final refund promise. The displayed settings are placeholders. Counsel and the payment provider must approve the timing, consumer rights, chargeback handling, seller settlement and refund wording before real payments are enabled.

Before payment

A renter may cancel an unpaid request through the booking. An owner may decline it. No rental payment or deposit is collected before the renter completes provider checkout.

After Gear payment and before pickup

The current application allows the renter to cancel a paid Gear booking only when at least the configured free-cancellation period remains before pickup. The default is 24 hours and is a placeholder. If the application accepts cancellation, it asks the provider to reverse seller transfers and refund the customer. Provider processing times, partial failures and payment-method rules can affect when a refund appears. The current application blocks cancellation inside the free window; a support-assisted exception process must be defined before launch.

After return

Both participants submit return photos. If the owner does not open a claim before the configured claim deadline, the app asks the provider to release the rental transfer, reverse the refundable deposit transfer and refund the remaining deposit. An assessed, settings-driven late fee may be routed to the owner from the deposit, capped by the deposit amount. The default claim window is 24 hours; all such values are placeholders.

When an owner raises a claim

The deposit stays on provider settlement hold while the renter can submit a response. An authorized reviewer compares the submitted claim and handover/return evidence with the conversation and booking history. The reviewer can return the full deposit, approve a partial deduction for the owner and return the balance, or release the full deposit to the owner. The rent is handled separately from the refundable deposit. An unresolved provider action is retried against the saved decision and recorded for reconciliation.

Provider timing and disputes

A NeighbourLink status update does not mean a bank transfer or refund has already settled. The payment provider controls processing and settlement timing. Customers may also have rights under their payment method and applicable law. Add provider-specific timelines, escalation contacts, chargeback handling and statutory remedies after legal and provider review.

Hire services

The current Hire portal records requests and completion but does not use the protected Gear checkout flow. A separate service cancellation, non-performance, rescheduling and refund policy must be agreed before service payments are introduced.
