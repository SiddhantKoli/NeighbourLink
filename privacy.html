POLICY DRAFT / 02

Privacy

Working product draft · 15 November 2026

Not a final privacy notice. Counsel and a privacy specialist must review this draft against actual processors, retention periods, user rights, security controls and applicable law before launch.

Information this product uses

  • Account information such as name, email address, phone number, profile image and sign-in/session records.
  • Listings, approximate listing location, saved items, booking dates, conversations, reviews and reports.
  • Payment order references, amounts, provider status and append-only money records. Card and bank credentials are handled by the payment provider.
  • Booking photos submitted at handover, return and claim time.
  • If you choose to submit identity verification, an ID image and face selfie. The app encrypts these images before private storage and restricts reviewer access. NeighbourLink does not request an Aadhaar number in the current flow.

Location

The browser asks for location permission only when you select a location feature. Approximate coordinates help calculate nearby results. If you publish a listing with coordinates, this version rounds them before saving and uses them for distance search. The neighbourhood text on a listing is visible to signed-in members. Review the actual precision and disclosure needed for launch.

How information is used

Information supports account access, local search, booking conversations, trust and safety review, provider payment instructions, transaction records, customer support, and email notices about requests, messages and booking changes. Identity documents are for the configured manual review workflow and are not shown publicly.

Sharing

Members see listing details and the booking information needed to interact. Booking participants can view private evidence for their booking. Authorized admins can review claim evidence, conversation history, KYC status and money records; each access to an ID or selfie image is recorded. After confirmed payment, booking contact details are available to the two participants. Service providers may receive the minimum information needed to process payment, settlement or required verification.

Storage, security and retention

Account and marketplace records are stored in the configured database. Images use private object storage. Identity images are encrypted by the application. This code currently schedules deletion of reviewed ID/selfie documents after a settings-driven retention period (90 days by default); pending cases remain until reviewed. Security safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee that a system is never compromised. Backups may retain data according to their own retention schedule.

Your choices and requests

You can decline browser location permission and search by neighbourhood. You can update account details and choose whether to submit optional KYC documents. Before production, publish a verified privacy contact and a tested process for access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, account closure and complaints where applicable.

Processors and international transfers

Configured deployments may use hosting, database, object storage, email, SMS and payment providers. List the actual provider legal names, regions, transfer safeguards and retention terms here before collecting real user data.
